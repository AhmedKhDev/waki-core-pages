What WAKI Core is

WAKI Core is the management app of Wakid Pharmaceutical Industries: products, registrations, countries, HR and the company's other day-to-day records, with KAI, an assistant that answers questions about them. It is for the company's own staff.

Getting an account

There is no public sign-up. Ask a WAKI Core admin at the company to create your account. The admin tells you how to sign in for the first time; after that you sign in with your email address and password.

Installing the app

The app is not listed in any store's search; open it from the link the company sends you.

  • iPhone, iPad and Mac: open the App Store link and tap Get.
  • Android: first send the email address of your Play Store account to the company, so it can add you to the app's testers. Then open the Play Store link, tap "Become a tester", and install the app from the same page.
  • Windows: open the Microsoft Store link and choose Install. The Store keeps the app up to date.

If the app says an update is required, tap the button it shows to open the store and update.

Help

For a problem with the app or your account, write to reg.export@wakipharma.com with what you were doing and, if you can, a screenshot.
