WAKI Core Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-26

WAKI Core is the internal management app of Wakid Pharmaceutical Industries. This policy says what the app collects, why, who processes it for us, how long we keep it and how to have it deleted.

Who is responsible

Wakid Pharmaceutical Industries is the controller of the data WAKI Core collects. Questions about this policy go to reg.export@wakipharma.com.

Who can use the app

WAKI Core is for the company's own staff. Only people given an account by a WAKI Core admin can sign in; there is no public sign-up, and the app offers nothing to anyone without an account.

What we collect and why

Everything below is used only to make the app work for the people who use it. It is linked to the account that entered or caused it.

DataWhy
Personal information: your name, email address, phone number and user ID To sign you in, show who did what, and reach you through the app.
Employment records that HR staff type in about people (for example contracts and attendance) To run the company's HR work inside the app, visible only to the roles allowed to see it.
Photos and files you upload To keep the documents and pictures that belong to the company's records.
KAI's messages: the questions you ask KAI, the app's assistant, and its answers To answer your question. KAI asks for your permission once before your first message is sent to its model provider.
Crash diagnostics: what the app was doing when an error happened, linked to your user ID, with your IP address removed To find and fix errors.
Your device's push token To deliver the app's notifications to your device.

The app collects nothing else: no location, no contacts, no advertising identifier and no browsing history.

Who processes it for us

These services handle the data above on our behalf, only to provide the app. All data travels encrypted (HTTPS).

  • Supabase: the app's database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Sentry: crash diagnostics.
  • Google Firebase: delivering push notifications.
  • Groq, or Anthropic when the company sets KAI to one of its models: the model provider that answers KAI's messages.

Not sold, not shared, not tracked

Your data is not sold, not shared with anyone for their own purposes, and not used for tracking or advertising. The app contains no advertising or analytics trackers.

How long we keep it

  • Account details and the company's records (including employment records, photos and files) are kept while they are part of the company's records, and deleted when the company no longer needs them or on request.
  • The text of KAI's messages is deleted after 30 days; a record of each question without its text, used to enforce usage limits, after 90 days.
  • Crash diagnostics are deleted by Sentry within 90 days.
  • The push token is removed when you sign out, or replaced when your device issues a new one.

Deleting your data

To have your account and the data linked to it deleted, write to reg.export@wakipharma.com from the address on your account. We will confirm when it is done. Records the company must keep by law are kept for the period the law requires.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the date above changes with it. For any question about your data, write to reg.export@wakipharma.com.
